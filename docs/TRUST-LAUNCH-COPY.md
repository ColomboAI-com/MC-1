## Product explanation

AI systems increasingly act through tools, credentials, memory, data stores, and other agents. That creates a deployment trust problem beyond model quality alone. MC-1 Trust is being built to connect Agent Identity, Agent Guard, runtime enforcement, AATS requirements, evidence, and continuous assurance. The proposed Trust Passport and Registry would let authorized relying parties inspect current scope and status rather than rely on an undated badge.

AATS is the **AI & Agentic Trust Standard**, a public working draft designed for use with or without MC-1. It defines the requirements; MC-1 Trust is ColomboAI's implementation in development.

## Founder social post

AI is becoming autonomous. Agents can hold credentials, call tools, access data, and act across systems. A model evaluation is essential, but it cannot by itself prove that a deployed agent is authorized or still operating within its boundaries.

We are developing MC-1 Trust: Continuous AI Trust Infrastructure. Alongside it, AATS (AI & Agentic Trust Standard) is being prepared as an open public working draft that others can implement independently.

Our principle is simple: every AI should be identifiable, every agent accountable, every action authorized, and every AI system continuously verifiable.

The next step is to publish the draft controls and demonstrate the evidence behind each product claim. We welcome technical critique from security teams, developers, privacy specialists, auditors, and independent evaluators.
